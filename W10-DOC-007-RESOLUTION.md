# W10-DOC-007 Resolution: Seed-round components sum to $3.7M but are labeled $2-3M

## Issue

**ID**: W10-DOC-007
**Area**: spec/contradiction
**Status**: RESOLVED

The seed-round breakdown lists Phase 1 = $2.0M and Growth (Phase 2–3) = $1.7M
(sum $3.7M), while the total was stated as "$2–3M". The stated $3M ceiling is
below the sum of the itemized line items and leaves no capital for the
separately listed $1.7M growth need.

## Resolution

The seed-round total is **$3.7M**, matching the itemized components.

| Phase | Stage | Amount |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Phase 1 | Seed | $2.0M |
| Phase 2–3 | Growth | $1.7M |
| **Total Seed Round** | | **$3.7M** |
