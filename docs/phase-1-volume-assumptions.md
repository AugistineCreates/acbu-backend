# Phase 1 Volume Assumptions

## 16. Phase 1 baseline

Phase 1 launches across three countries with a total volume target of **$20M**.

## 19.4 Sensitivity: two-country launch

If Phase 1 launches in two of the three planned countries, the sensitivity case
uses the same per-country volume assumption as the baseline:

```text
$20M x (2 / 3) = $13.33M
```

The two-country sensitivity volume target is therefore **$13.33M**, rounded to
the nearest tenth of a million. It must not be interpreted as a larger target
than the full three-country baseline.
