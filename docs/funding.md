# ACBU Seed Round Funding

The seed round is itemized into a Phase 1 raise and a Phase 2–3 growth tranche.
The round total is the sum of those components.

## Allocation

| Phase | Stage | Amount |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Phase 1 | Seed | $2.0M |
| Phase 2–3 | Growth | $1.7M |
| **Total Seed Round** | | **$3.7M** |

## Reconciliation

- Phase 1: **$2.0M**
- Growth (Phase 2–3): **$1.7M**
- Total Seed Round: **$3.7M** ($2.0M + $1.7M)

The seed-round total is **$3.7M**, i.e. the sum of the itemized components. An
earlier "$2–3M" figure was inconsistent with this breakdown — its $3M upper
bound was below the sum of the line items and left no capital for the separately
listed $1.7M growth tranche — and is superseded by the $3.7M total above.
